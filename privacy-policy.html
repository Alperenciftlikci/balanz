Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

The short version. Your health data — calories, weight, steps, workouts — is read from Apple Health, stays on your iPhone, and is never uploaded to our servers. Balanz has no accounts, no ads, and never sells your data.

1. Who we are

Balanz ("the app") is developed by Alperen Çiftlikçi. You can reach us any time at ciftlikcialperen@gmail.com.

2. Health and fitness data

With your permission, Balanz reads the following from Apple Health: active and resting energy, dietary energy and macronutrients, water, body mass, body fat percentage, steps, exercise minutes, workouts, activity rings, resting heart rate, and your date of birth, biological sex and height.

With your permission, Balanz also writes the food, water and weight entries you add in the app back to Apple Health, so your other apps stay in sync.

This data is processed on your device and stored on your device. It is not transmitted to our servers, not backed up to our infrastructure, and never used for advertising or sold to anyone. Deleting the app removes the copy Balanz holds; data in Apple Health itself is controlled by you in the Health app.

3. What does leave your device

Balanz uses Google Firebase for a small amount of non-health data:

  • Anonymous identifier. Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a random ID so your subscription can be restored. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID.
  • Subscription status. Whether your Balanz Pro subscription is active, plus the date your record was created and whether you consented to AI features.
  • App Check. A device-integrity token that prevents abuse of our backend.

The app also communicates with RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and with the App Store to process purchases. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card information.

4. AI features (optional, opt-in)

Balanz offers two optional AI features powered by Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic:

  • Weekly insight. Only aggregate weekly numbers are sent — average calories eaten and burned, average steps, number of logged days, weight change and your goal. Individual meals, workouts and raw Apple Health samples are never sent. This runs only after you explicitly agree, and you can turn it off at any time in the app.
  • AI calorie estimate. When you tap "Estimate calories with AI", only the food description you typed is sent (for example "two eggs and toast"). No health data accompanies it.

Google processes these requests on our behalf and, under Firebase AI Logic terms, does not use them to train its models. If you never enable AI features, nothing is sent. The app works fully without them.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Balanz sends local reminders — an evening check-in, weigh-in prompts and a weekly report. These are scheduled on your device; no notification content is sent through our servers.

6. Data we do not collect

Balanz does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

7. Children

Balanz is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Revoke Apple Health access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Balanz.
  • Turn AI features off in the app at any time.
  • Use Delete account in the app to erase your anonymous record from our backend and clear local data.
  • Email ciftlikcialperen@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of any data associated with your anonymous ID.

9. Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete it or remove the app. The anonymous backend record is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you use Delete account.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

11. Not medical advice

Balanz provides estimates for general wellness and fitness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.
