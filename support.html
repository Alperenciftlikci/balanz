Balanz doesn't see my burned calories

Balanz needs read access to Apple Health. Open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Balanz and make sure Active Energy, Resting Energy, Steps and Workouts are enabled. If you don't wear an Apple Watch, Balanz estimates movement from your iPhone's step count instead.

Why is my target different from other apps?

Balanz builds your target from a standard formula (Mifflin-St Jeor by default) plus your goal pace, and the deficit or surplus is already included — you simply eat up to the number. After a few weeks of consistent logging and weigh-ins, adaptive targets replace the formula with what your body actually does, so the number may shift.

My food from another app isn't showing

Balanz reads dietary energy from Apple Health. Make sure the other app is set to write food data to Health, and that Balanz has permission to read it. Entries appear after the next refresh.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Profile → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Balanz. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; you keep access until that period is over. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Use Profile → Delete account in the app. This removes the anonymous record from our backend and clears local data. Data stored in Apple Health itself is managed by you in the Health app.

Is Balanz medical advice?

No. Balanz gives estimates for general wellness and is not a medical device. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet or training.
