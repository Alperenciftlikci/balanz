Your health data stays yours

Balanz was built so that the sensitive part never leaves your iPhone.

  • ✓Health data never leaves your device. Calories, weight, steps and workouts are read from Apple Health and stored locally — they are never uploaded to our servers.
  • ✓No account, no sign-up. Balanz uses an anonymous ID so nothing is tied to your name or email.
  • ✓No ads, no data selling. We do not sell or share your information, and we never use health data for marketing.
  • ✓AI features are opt-in. The weekly insight only sends anonymous weekly totals, and only after you agree. You can turn it off anytime.
